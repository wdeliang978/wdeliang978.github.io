About

I study how advanced technologies can support STEM and language education, teacher education, and educational equity.

My research brings together AI, learning analytics, explainable AI, and educational technology to understand and improve teaching and learning. I develop and evaluate AI tools for analyzing interactions in classroom dialogue, collaborative discussion, and online tutoring, and for supporting teacher professional development, STEM education, and language learning. Across these areas, I am interested in how technology can advance educational equity.

I received my PhD in AI in Education from the University of Hong Kong in 2026, following an MPhil in Educational Technology at Beijing Normal University.

From December 2026, I will join the Chinese University of Hong Kong as an Assistant Professor.

Research

Interaction analysis
Using machine learning, language models, and sequence analysis to study social interactions in K-12 and higher education, including collaborative learning and online tutoring.
AI & explainable AI
Developing AI models for educational analysis and feedback, and examining how explanations help teachers interpret model outputs, assess their reliability, and reflect on their teaching.
AI-supported STEM & language learning
Designing and evaluating AI-supported curricula and learning activities in STEM and language education, including mathematics learning and EFL writing.
Educational equity
Investigating how AI-supported teaching and inclusive curriculum design can expand learning opportunities for students in under-resourced settings and for ethnic minority learners.
